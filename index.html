Records MIDI notes, timing, velocity and duration—not keyboard audio. A 1–2–3 count-in comes before recording and playback, with a tempo click throughout. Maximum 2 minutes of recording after the count-in. Sustain pedal (CC64), volume (CC7) and expression (CC11) are recorded. Pedal sound is on/off, not a half-pedal acoustic model. Other controllers are ignored. Connect MIDI below, then Done to record. Opening a sheet stops playback/practice and safely finishes an active recording.

BPM is set by you, not detected. Playback speed = playback BPM ÷ recorded BPM.Rhythm checks note onsets, not release lengths. Chords group nearby notes; all chord tones must be played. A wrong note restarts after you release all keys and a four-beat count-in.

MIDI keyboard

Use Chrome or Edge on your Mac with the keyboard connected by USB. YC Series Port 1 is preferred when available. Play the shown octave for memory, builder and dictionary exercises. The screen starts at MIDI note 48 (C below middle C); middle C is MIDI 60. Keyboard octave labels can differ. Release keys before the next question. MIDI is paused while a sheet is open. Your keyboard’s own sound may play alongside the app; KeyMemory sends no MIDI output and changes no keyboard settings.
