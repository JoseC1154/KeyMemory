MIDI keyboard

Use Chrome or Edge on your Mac with the keyboard connected by USB. YC Series Port 1 is preferred when available. Play the shown octave for memory, builder and dictionary exercises. The screen starts at MIDI note 48 (C below middle C); middle C is MIDI 60. Keyboard octave labels can differ. Release keys before the next question. MIDI is paused while a sheet is open. Your keyboard’s own sound may play alongside the app; KeyMemory sends no MIDI output and changes no keyboard settings.
